# Supplementary video legends

**Supplementary Video 1 | Case 1 method comparison.** Density response
and polarity magnitude from particles, the polar PDE and the Fourier solution
from $t=0$ to $10^{-3}$. Each observable retains one linear color scale
throughout. Playback is 10 s at 30 frames s⁻¹.

and polarity magnitude for the eight remaining mechanisms from $t=0$ to

case and frame. Both videos use the same density range, polarity range and
spatial window. Playback is 10 s at 30 frames s⁻¹.
